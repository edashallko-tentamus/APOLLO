# Ontology Principles

## Value ownership
Every attribute is classified as a [[User Value]], [[Derived Value]], or [[System Value]].

## Object classifications
Objects are classified as [[Definition Object]], [[Execution Object]], [[Event Object]], or [[Governance Object]].

## Design principles
- Business objects represent concepts with independent lifecycles.
- Capabilities represent business functions rather than software screens.
- A [[Test]] is the orderable, report-facing identity; the analytical method is [[Method]], and scientific content is versioned on [[Test Version]].
- Each [[Test Version]] owns an approved [[Test Workflow]].
- A [[Requested Test]] creates a [[Test Workflow Instance]].
- Operational reality is recorded even when it creates exceptions, such as [[Negative Inventory]].
- Controlled corrections create new events rather than rewriting history.
- AI may explain and recommend, but regulated approvals remain controlled by [[Role]], [[Permission]], and [[Electronic Signature]].
- Every data grid supports fill down and copy/paste ([[Grid Data Entry Standard]]).
- Every orderable test traces to an accepted quoted line ([[Quoted-Only Ordering Rule]]).
- GxP record controls are enforced by the database ([[Decision - GxP Record Controls Are Enforced by the Database]]).
