# Invoice Claim Rule

Completed work is claimed onto the claimer's own Draft invoice for its lab order. Nobody else claims it, changes the draft or issues it until the holder issues or releases it; a lab administrator may take a draft back with a reason. Claiming is recorded, not signed.

Enforced in the database (migration 036). See [[Decision - Invoicing I-1]]. Applies to [[Invoice]] and [[Invoice Line Item]].
