# Late Rush Billing Rule

A rush test reported after its due date shows how many working days late it was. The reviewer may bill the rush tier ordered, a tier from the client's quote whose turnaround (from the test's Day 0) was met, or standard; never a tier dearer than ordered, and with a reason printed on the invoice when it differs from the order.

Enforced in the database (migration 036). See [[Decision - Invoicing I-1]]. Applies to [[Invoice]] and [[Invoice Line Item]].
