# Decision - Chemical List Is Maintained by Supervisors, QA, GMs and Administrators

> **Superseded 2026-10-12** by [[Decision - Chemical List Retired, Inventory Items Name Their Analytes]]. Kept as history.

Lab Supervisors, QA staff, General Managers and administrators can add and change entries on the controlled [[Chemical]] list (name and CAS number; CAS unique). Analysts cannot. Until full [[Role]] and [[Permission]] control is built, the Sandbox records a simple job role on each user to enforce this. Resolves the open question in [[Decision - Chemical Inventory Unified Under Inventory Lot]].

See [[Ontology Principles]].
