# Decision - Client Account Status Is Active, On Hold or Inactive

Client status has three values. On Hold lets the lab log samples and test, but reports cannot be released or sent. Inactive blocks new orders but still allows reports for work already in the lab. Prospect was dropped. See [[Client Account Status Rule]].
