# Decision - Rush Fees Are Quoted Per Tier With Their Own Discount

Rush schedules are copied onto the quote when issued and listed at the bottom with their tiers. The rush surcharge applies to the client's final price (or is flat), and each tier carries its own discount percent on the quote ([[Quote Rush Term]]), so Same Day can stay full price while a 5 Day rush is free; a line can override a tier. Dollars always shown. See [[Rush Fee Calculation Rule]].
